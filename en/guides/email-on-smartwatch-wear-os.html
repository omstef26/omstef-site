How to get your email on your smartwatch

Galaxy Watch, Pixel Watch, TicWatch, Fossil: which smartwatches can read email on the wrist, and how to set it up.

The short answer

To read email on an Android smartwatch you need a watch running Wear OS 3 or newer and an email app that runs on the watch. Notifications alone are not enough: they vanish when your phone is out of range and you cannot browse the inbox. WatchMail is a standalone email client: read, delete and move messages right from your wrist.

Which smartwatches are compatible?

Any watch running Wear OS 3 or newer:

Step by step

  1. Install WatchMail from Google Play (installed from your phone, the app is also pushed to the watch).
  2. Open the app on your phone and add your account: Gmail, Outlook/Hotmail, Yahoo, iCloud or any IMAP/POP3 account.
  3. Gmail, Yahoo and iCloud require an app password instead of your usual password.
  4. Your mail appears on the watch. Delete, move or mark as read with a single gesture.

FAQ

How do I get my email on my smartwatch?

With a Wear OS 3+ watch, install a standalone email app such as WatchMail, add your account, and the inbox opens on the watch.

Does it work on Apple Watch, Garmin or Huawei?

No, only on Android watches running Wear OS 3 or newer.

Must the phone stay close?

No. After setup the watch connects by itself over Wi-Fi or LTE, depending on the model.
