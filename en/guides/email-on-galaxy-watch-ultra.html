How-to guide

Read email on Galaxy Watch Ultra / Watch 8

Models covered: Galaxy Watch Ultra, Galaxy Watch 7 and Galaxy Watch 8. Here is how to read your mail on your wrist, even without your phone.

What you need

  • A Galaxy Watch Ultra / Watch 8 (Wear OS 3 or newer).
  • An IMAP or POP3 account.
  • The WatchMail app.

Steps

  1. Install WatchMail from Google Play.
  2. Add your account on the phone. Gmail needs an app password.
  3. Your mail shows on the watch: delete, move or mark as read with one gesture.

Other brands: compatible watches.

FAQ

Can I read email on a Galaxy Watch Ultra / Watch 8?

Yes. Galaxy Watch Ultra / Watch 8 runs Wear OS: install WatchMail, add your IMAP or POP3 account and the inbox appears on the watch.

Which email accounts are supported?

IMAP and POP3 accounts: Gmail (app password), Yahoo, Orange and others.
