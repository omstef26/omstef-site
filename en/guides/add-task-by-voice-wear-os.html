How-to guide

Add a Microsoft To Do task by voice on your watch

An idea while walking? Dictate it on your wrist and it lands in Microsoft To Do.

How to

  1. Install NoTasTick on your phone (the watch follows).
  2. Tap "Sign in with Microsoft" (official Microsoft page, no password typed in the app).
  3. On the watch tap "Add" and say: "Call the dentist tomorrow".
  4. The task is created with the due date recognised.

More

  • Complete or reopen a task with one gesture.
  • Edit due date and importance, manage steps.
  • "Planned" view grouped by due date.
  • Offline mode: actions sync when you reconnect.

Watches: compatible Wear OS list.

FAQ

Can I add a Microsoft To Do task by voice on a Galaxy Watch?

Yes, with NoTasTick: dictate the task on the watch; words like tomorrow, tonight or a weekday become the due date.

Do I need the official Microsoft To Do app?

No. NoTasTick connects directly to your Microsoft account.
