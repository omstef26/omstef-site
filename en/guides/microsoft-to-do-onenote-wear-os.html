Microsoft To Do and OneNote on your Wear OS smartwatch

Galaxy Watch, Pixel Watch, TicWatch: check tasks, add them by voice and read OneNote notes from your wrist.

The short answer

Microsoft has no official To Do or OneNote app for Wear OS. NoTasTick fills the gap: sign in with your Microsoft account once, then check, add and complete Microsoft To Do tasks and read your OneNote notes from your wrist. After the first sign-in the watch works on its own.

Not compatible: Apple Watch, Garmin, Huawei (HarmonyOS), Amazfit and Galaxy Watch 3 and older (Tizen). NoTasTick is tested on Galaxy Watch; other Wear OS 3+ models should work but are not all tested.

What you can do

  • Browse your To Do lists, with due dates and importance.
  • Add a task by typing or by voice, and complete it with one tap.
  • Read OneNote notebooks, sections and pages, with colours and formatting, even offline.
  • Edit OneNote pages with a rich editor on the phone.

FAQ

Is there a Microsoft To Do app for Wear OS?

Not an official one. NoTasTick is an independent app that connects to your Microsoft account.

Which smartwatches work?

Wear OS 3+ watches: Galaxy Watch 4 and later, Pixel Watch, TicWatch, Fossil Gen 6 and more.

Voice tasks?

Yes, dictate a task and "tomorrow" or "tonight" become due dates.
