Microsoft To Do and OneNote on Galaxy Watch Ultra / Watch 8

Models covered: Galaxy Watch Ultra, Galaxy Watch 7 and Galaxy Watch 8. Your tasks and notes on your wrist.

What you can do

  • Browse Microsoft To Do lists and tasks, with due dates and importance.
  • Add a task by voice.
  • Read OneNote pages, even offline.

Get started

  1. Install NoTasTick from Google Play.
  2. Sign in with Microsoft on your phone.
  3. Open NoTasTick on the watch.

See also voice entry and the full guide. NoTasTick is independent and not affiliated with Microsoft.

FAQ

Is there Microsoft To Do on Galaxy Watch Ultra / Watch 8?

There is no official Microsoft app for Wear OS. NoTasTick shows your Microsoft To Do tasks and OneNote notes on Galaxy Watch Ultra / Watch 8.

Do I need my phone?

Only for the first Microsoft sign-in; after that the watch works on its own.
