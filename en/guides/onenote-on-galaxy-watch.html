OneNote on your Galaxy Watch

A shopping list or recipe in OneNote? Read it on your wrist without taking out your phone.

What you can do

  • Browse OneNote notebooks, sections and pages.
  • Keep formatting and colours.
  • Read offline thanks to the local copy.
  • Create and edit pages on the phone.

Get started

  1. Install NoTasTick.
  2. Sign in with Microsoft on your phone.
  3. Open your notes on the watch.

FAQ

Can I read OneNote on a Galaxy Watch?

Yes, with NoTasTick: notebooks, sections and pages with their formatting, even offline thanks to a local copy.

Can I create a OneNote page from the watch?

The watch is for reading. Creating and editing pages is done on the phone with the rich editor.
