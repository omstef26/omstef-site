Gmail on Galaxy Watch: read your email on your wrist

Galaxy Watch models (4, 5, 6, 7, Ultra) have no full standalone Gmail app. Here is how to read your Gmail right on the watch.

Why doesn't Gmail just work?

On Wear OS, Gmail is mostly limited to notifications. To browse, delete and move messages without your phone you need an email client that runs on the watch, such as WatchMail.

Steps

  1. Turn on 2-step verification in your Google account (Security).
  2. Create an "app password" (16 letters), for example named "Watch".
  3. In WatchMail on your phone, add a Gmail account with imap.gmail.com (port 993, SSL) and that app password.
  4. Your mail shows up on the Galaxy Watch.

Compatible watches: Wear OS 3+ list.

FAQ

How do I get Gmail on my Galaxy Watch?

Install a standalone email client such as WatchMail, turn on 2-step verification in your Google account, create an app password and enter it instead of your normal password.

Why does Gmail reject my password on the watch?

Google does not accept your regular password in third-party email apps: you need a 16-letter app password.
